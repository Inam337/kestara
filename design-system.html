06 · MOTION & ACCESSIBILITY

Motion

160ms easeColour and border transitions on hover and focus.
550ms cubicLifecycle rail marker and progress fill — cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1).
700ms riseSection and hero entrance: 16px translate with fade.
2.4s pulseLive status dots on the badge and lifecycle ticker.
reduced-motionAll animation and autoplay disabled when the user prefers reduced motion.

Accessibility rules

Body text holds 4.5:1 contrast; headline-scale type holds at least 3:1.
Visible 2px blue focus ring with 2px offset on every interactive element.
One H1 per page with an ordered H2/H3 hierarchy.
Form fields use real labels, required indicators and inline error text.
Status is never conveyed by colour alone — a label always accompanies the dot.
Decorative SVG is aria-hidden; the logo mark carries an accessible name.
